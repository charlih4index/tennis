Q:  When do you play?  For how long?
A:  We play regularly every Sunday afternoon, except announced otherwise.  The starting time varies from season to season and we usually play until after sunset.  For Summer, it is from 4:30PM to 8PM or so.  We usually go have dinner together afterwards.
 

Q:  Can I join if I'm totally new to tennis?
A:  You are welcome to join us even if you have never played before, or you are just a beginner.   Actually, in order to help you learn how to play tennis, every week we have a voluntee coach to give you instructions on basic strokes.  The training session starts at 4:30PM in the training area, which is behind the tennis courts.  There's no need to sign up to take the lesson.  Just show up at the training area on time and our coach will give you instructions.
