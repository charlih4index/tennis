     Frequently Asked Questions about The Tennis Group:

Q: Where do you play?  How do I get there?
A: We play at the tennis courts of Lynbrook High School.  The 
address is:

Lynbrook High School
1280 Johnson Avenue
San Jose, California 95129

For a map, please go to:
http://maps.yahoo.com/py/maps.py?Pyt=Tmap&addr=1280+Johnson+Ave&csz=San+Jose%2C+CA+95129&Get+Map=Get+Map

Here's the directions:
 . From I-280, take the Lawrence Expressway exit and go south
 . Drive about 200 yards on Lawrence Expressway
 . Turn right onto Bollinger Road, drive about 200 yards
 . Turn left onto Johnson Drive, and drive a few blocks
 . Turn left into the parking lot of Lynbrook High when you see a 
stop sign which is Rainbow Drive.


Q:  When do you play?  For how long?
A:  We play regularly every Sunday afternoon, except announced 
otherwise.  The starting time varies from season to season and we 
usually play until after sunset.  For Summer, it is from 5:00PM to 
8PM or so.  We usually go have dinner together afterwards.


Q:  Can I join if I'm totally new to tennis?
A:  You are welcome to join us even if you have never played 
before, or you are just a beginner.   Actually, in order to help you 
learn how to play tennis, every week we have a volunteer coach to 
give you instructions on basic strokes.  The training session starts 
at 6:00PM in the training area, which is behind the tennis courts.  
There's no need to sign up to take the lesson.  Just show up at the 
training area on time and our coach will give you instructions.
