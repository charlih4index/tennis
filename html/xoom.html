Apply free web page on www.xoom.com (Step by Step)
by Charlih Chen on June 28, 1999
[My goal is that let each member has their own web page]
  1. Open IE5 or Netscape browser
  2. Type URL at http://www.xoom.com
  3. Click where red in circled.

  4. Click on JOIN NOW!
  5. Keying your current eMail address -> Click on continue ...
  6. Choose Member name: (which is your login name)
  7. Choose your password
  8. Input your personal information...Address/Occupation/Gender/....
  9. Click on JOIN
  10. ==================================================
  11. Go ahead and check mail from your current eMail
  12. You should get email from xoom.com with activate URL
  13. Click on the URL to activate your free web page on xoom.com
  14. Your web page will be http://members.xoom.com/your_member_name
  15. ==================================================
  16. The main web page is called index.html
  17. I usually use Netscape editor to create web page:
    • Browse a web page that you like, click edit page
    • Modifying the page -> save it as index.html to your local C: driver
    • By using FTP to upload index.html to your account at ftp.xoom.com
