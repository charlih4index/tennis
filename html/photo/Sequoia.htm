Kings Canyon and Sequoia National Parks

If you have attended our previous show on May 14 for Yosemite trip,
you will see a totally different favor this time. Instead of rivers and giant falls, we
are going to present you with magnificent mountains and giant ancient sequoia
and red woods. Can you image a tree who is older than Jesus and what it looks
like?  Will you wander trees whose trunk can accommodate a car or cars can
actually drive on top of the tree?
Seeing is believing. The tunnel log and auto log are going to answer that.
Would you like to experience "back to the future"? Without the working
antique, gas pump from 1928, we would be trapped in the mountain for a night.
(written by Yen-Huang Lin)

You are invited to enjoy those wonderful moments with us.
