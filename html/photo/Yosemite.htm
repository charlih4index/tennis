Yosemite National Park

On the Memorial weekend, a few people went to visited Yosemite Valley. Yosemite Valley is famous for its impressive waterfalls, cliffs, and unusual rock formations. You probably heard about them. I did not believe until I saw them. Now we have a few slides to show the astonished beauty of Yosemite Valley. If you have been to the valley before, it may be a good time to refresh your memory. In case you did not leave your trace in the valley, it is a chance to preview the magic of nature.

A few highlight of the slides include:
Half Dome, Yosemite's most distinctive feature, dominates the valleys view. El Capitan, a massive granite monolith rises 3593 feet from the valley floor. About a month ago, an eighty years old man spent two weeks (?) to climb to the top and broke his own record and became the oldest man ever climb to the top. Yosemite Falls, composes of the upper and lower falls.  Vernal Fall, Nevada Fall, the Mist Trail and the Merced River, consumed us a whole day to hike the whole path. We felt it is well worth the time and effort.
(Written by Yen-Huang Lin)
