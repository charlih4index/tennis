Invitation and Registration for Mid-Summer Event of Bay Area Tennis Club
Cupertino

For many of fun lovers and tennis players, Bay Area Tennis Club Cupertino
welcomes you to join us in the Mid-Summer BBQ and Tennis tournament event on

people like you together.  Enjoy the yam-mee taste BBQ from professional BBQ
